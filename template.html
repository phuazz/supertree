How are these paths made?

A made-up path is like rolling a special dice once a month. Most rolls nudge your money up or down a little; now and then a roll brings a big jump or a big drop. On average the rolls add up to the growth you picked, and over a year they swing about as much as this fund really has since 2008. The made-up line is one set of monthly rolls — 120 rolls for 10 years. The page rolls 2,000 sets: the funnel shows where most of them end up, and “Show another” picks a different set.

A real past stretch is not made up at all. It replays what this fund actually did, month by month (with dividends put back in), as if you had started then with the same money. Your growth setting does not change it — that is what really happened.

Why both? Made-up rolls are fair, but each month is rolled on its own. Real markets have calm spells and stormy spells that bunch together — in 2008 the bad months came one after another. The real stretch shows that.
